# 2026-11-21 · Product & UI Strategy, Wireframes, Feature Registry & Version-Aware Steward

## Context & Goal
Across PRs `#10–#35`, Kevin and JT shipped a 5-stage Managed Agent pipeline and two parallel UI stacks — a vanilla-JS SPA on `/` (`simon/static/`) and 7 Python SSR pages (`simon/*_ui.py`) — without a shared wireframe, user-story, or route-ownership document. That lack of a canonical product blueprint triggered a 5-PR UI convergence loop (`#27, #30, #31, #32, #34`) and an immediate post-unification split-brain in PR `#35` (where Source Hunter, 42 sources across 8 US regions, the `American Idol · Nationwide Search` preset, and `Run Full Pipeline Cascade` landed on SSR `/discovery` while topbar tab `1. Discovery` still pointed to stale SPA `/?tab=discovery`).

An 8-worker swarm audited 60 Antigravity sessions (`22` root chats, `38` subagents), full git/PR history (`#1–#35`), all 8 HTTP routes + 4 SPA tabs, 12 `agents/` directories, and `117/117` passing unit tests to establish `pm/product/` and upgrade Steward governance.
