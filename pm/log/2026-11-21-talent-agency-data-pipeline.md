- **Confirmed North-Star Architectural Thesis ("An Actual Talent Agency Driven Fully by Agents")**:
  - Defined Simon's 4-stage progressive data and arbitration architecture separating **Plane A (Progressive Talent Dossier Enrichment, Stages 1–3)** from **Plane B (Mandate-Driven "Taste" & Standout Pipeline, Stage 4)** linked by closed-loop `EnrichmentTask` jobs:
    1. **Stage 1 — Discovery & Basic Profile (`Bronze`, Zero Media Digestion)**: Fast entity resolution (`art_*`), platform handles, `21+` / US work-authorization / metro gates, `local://` PII vault pointers, and queued `MediaAssetRef[]` URIs without spending audio/video tokens.
    2. **Stage 2 — Metric-Blind Multimodal Media Digestion (`Silver`)**: Zero-trust (`network: "disabled"`) `gemini-4-hydrogen` + Python DSP (`scipy`/`ffmpeg`) digestion auditing AI-slop/lip-sync/Melodyne crutches, `live_vs_studio_vocal_delta`, lyric specificity, clickable `MM:SS` observations, and live room chatter drop ("bar hush effect") strictly blinded to follower/stream counts.
    3. **Stage 3 — Sparsity-Aware Scout Metadata Enrichment (`Gold`)**: Explicit `data_footprint_regime` (`pre_dsp_underground` vs. `early_dsp_inflection`) so unreleased discoveries are never penalized for null DSP stats, enriching conversion ratios (`save_rate_pct`, `follower_to_listener_ratio_pct`), audience comment pull forensics, NYC Room Ladder (`Tier 0`–`Tier 3`) hard-ticket history, one-stop sync status, and representation whitespace (`UNAGENTED_NA_WITH_LIVE_DRAW`).
    4. **Stage 4 — Mandate-Driven "Taste" & Standout Arbitration**: Calibrated `DSPy` + `gemini-decision-1` zero-output-token gate followed by 4 epistemically isolated Managed Agent critics (`critic_vocal_artistry` kept strictly metric-blind, `critic_cultural_whitespace`, `validator_live_booking`, `critic_red_team_skeptic`) and `gemini-4-carbon` Partner/Producer synthesis preserving `peak_champion_score` + `critic_score_std_dev` and emitting `pending_human_review` offer packets or `EnrichmentTask` loops.
