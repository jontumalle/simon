# Scouting Sources, Calibration & Modular Verticals

*Distilled from the 2026-11-21 kickoff transcript.*

## 1. Multi-Source Discovery Channels
- **YouTube / SoundCloud / Instagram**: Scouting raw vocal takes, open-mic clips, and low-view uploads where strong vocalists haven't yet been surfaced by engagement algorithms.
- **Direct Opt-In Submissions**: Open-mic hosts and local scene calls (cleanest path for live pilot consent and guaranteed-fee offers).

## 2. Critic Calibration ("Breakout vs. Noise" Backtesting)
- Test Simon's critic agents on a mixed calibration batch containing:
  - Early pre-breakout raw clips of singers who later succeeded (e.g., pre-fame audition/cover takes).
  - Polished studio-heavy clips with unclear live execution.
  - Low-fit / unpolished control clips.
- Measure whether the `performance-critic` and `booking-critic` surface the right musical evidence and practical uncertainties without knowing artist names or follower counts.

## 3. Swappable Scenes & Verticals
- Because Simon separates **evidence ingestion**, **independent domain critics**, and **producer synthesis**, swapping the event brief and critic rubrics adapts the exact same Managed Agent graph to:
  - Hyper-local music scenes (e.g., Hudson County NJ, NYC listening rooms).
  - Other live talent showcases (comedy sets, spoken word, indie bands, DJs).

## 4. Downstream Producer / Label Services *(Post-Hackathon Stretch)*
- Once Simon identifies and books standout emerging vocalists on paid stages, future agents could assist consenting artists with live EP production, press kits, visual identity, and recurring showcase residencies.
